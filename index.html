Good to know

Common questions, straight answers.

Do you handle small repairs as well as full renovations?

Yes. We do everything from a leaky faucet or drywall patch to full kitchen remodeling, bathroom renovation, roofing, and whole-home remodeling. Whatever the size, you deal directly with the family that does the work.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Archats LLC is licensed, insured, and fully permitted for the work we take on. We're also a BBB accredited business and we've been handling home improvement projects in Queens for over 10 years.

Where do you work?

We're based in Queens and serve all of its neighborhoods — Astoria, Forest Hills, Flushing, Long Island City, Jackson Heights, Bayside and beyond — plus Long Island, Brooklyn, and Hudson and Bergen counties in New Jersey.

How does the estimate process work?

We visit your home, listen to what you're planning, and give you a detailed, itemized estimate — free, with no obligation. No guesswork, no surprise charges later.

How long does a kitchen or bathroom remodel take?

It depends on scope, but a typical bathroom renovation runs about one to two weeks and a full kitchen remodel about three to six. We lock in a timeline before we start and keep you updated daily.

Do you back up your work?

Yes. We walk through every detail together when the job is done and we stand behind the work after we leave. If something isn't right, we make it right.
